Vanta creates helmets where protection meets precision.

Built for the Hit

Every ride comes with the unexpected. Vanta’s protective construction is engineered with a durable outer shell and impact-absorbing interior designed to help manage energy during an impact, giving you an added layer of confidence wherever the road takes you.

See Every Move

Protection means nothing if you can’t see what’s coming. Our wide field of vision keeps your surroundings clear, while the precision visor helps reduce distractions from wind, debris, and changing conditions so you can stay focused on the road ahead.

Locked in Place

A helmet should move with you, not against you. Vanta’s secure fit system is designed to keep the helmet comfortably positioned around your head, minimizing unwanted movement while maintaining the stability and freedom you need through every turn.

Protection Without the Weight

Serious protection shouldn’t feel like a burden. Vanta combines lightweight construction with a carefully balanced profile to reduce unnecessary weight, helping you stay comfortable and focused for longer rides without sacrificing the feeling of security.
